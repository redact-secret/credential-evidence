## Readiness inventory

- **ready**: a provider-documented claim names the carrier (a header, query parameter, response member, form field or Basic password); the Case flags a value in that position.
- **carrier-unresolved**: the claims establish a role or existence only; no outcome is asserted and the open question is recorded.
- **policy-limited**: the carrier is named but an unsettled fact (an era end, a date conflict, a source conflict or the currency of an archived page) limits what the evidence can say; the expectation rests on project policy and states the limit.

A row can hold more than one status for different carriers.

## What was not authored, and why

- Adobe private key file text: no encoding, label or length is stated, so no value was constructed and the Case has no fixture. A PEM block would have put a generic private-key shape under an Adobe family.
- A positive Case for the Adobe signed assertion: Adobe states no enforced lifetime and does not say whether a captured assertion is sensitive, so it is recorded as unsettled, not as a non-value.
- Benign siblings that are not documented as public: the Reddit client id (`reddit-oauth-client-id`, class `unresolved`), the Dropbox `account_id` and `uid`, the Adobe identifiers' own confidentiality and the Zendesk email address are not asserted either way. Adobe identifiers are asserted only not to be the signing key.
- Reddit `expires_in` values (unit unresolved), the retirement and end-of-life dates of every row (provider announcements, revised or conflicting), and the last-working date of any credential: none is a Case-assertable input.
- The Zendesk URL userinfo form with `%2F`, and a JFrog base64 Basic header: the first is documented for HTTP authentication only; the second has no JFrog statement of the encoding step.
- No variant, contract revision, sibling, narrative or family edit. No Case was promoted to `reviewed`.

## Audit of the existing JFrog and Zendesk Cases (credential-evidence PR #253, E2)
